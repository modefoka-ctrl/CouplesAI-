Tips for Better Prompts
1 — Start with World & Tension
Step 2 opens automatically on every load. Search 50 worlds by typing in the search box or browse by category. Pick a tension — a poetic translation appears beneath it so you know the exact mood it creates. Tension unlocks the affinity system: curated locations, poses and looks matched to that emotional register appear at the top of every other dropdown.
2 — Tension is mood, not instruction
Every prompt ends with: "The emotional tension (X) is a mood and atmosphere directive only — do not illustrate it literally." AI models read "buried vs. exposed" and try to show someone literally buried. The directive prevents that.
3 — Lock & Spin Unlocked
Lock any field with the padlock — including World and Tension. Then hit Spin Unlocked to explore every combination within your locked anchor. Locks survive Randomize All and Clear.
4 — Keyboard shortcuts
Press / to jump to world search. Press 1 2 3 4 to toggle steps. Press Alt+R to randomize. Press Alt+C to copy the prompt. Press Escape to close any modal.
5 — ✦ AI decides
Every field has a ✦ AI button. Tap it to leave that field open to the model. Setting 2–3 fields manually and leaving 1–2 to AI often produces the strongest results.
6 — Randomize All opens every step
Hit ✦ Randomize All and all four steps open automatically showing what was selected. Lock what you like, then Spin Unlocked to explore variations. Alt+R randomizes from anywhere.
7 — Mood worlds auto-set lighting
Selecting DUSK, SPRING, ROMANCE, GOLD, NIGHT, RAIN or COLD automatically sets lighting to ✦ AI decides. Override manually if you want explicit control.
8 — Shot type front-loads composition
Shot type appears first in the prompt — image models weight early tokens most heavily. Extreme close-up forces focus on faces. Wide environmental makes the world as important as the subjects.
9 — Era as tonal modifier
Era appends a period and palette modifier. Tap the active pill again — or hit the ✕ clear button — to deselect. Use era when a prompt feels too generic.
10 — Coherence badge
Green ✦ Cohesive / amber ◐ Moderate / red ○ Scattered scores your combination against the tension's affinity map. Tap or hover to see why. Scattered can produce more distinctive images.
11 — Shareable links
Every selection encodes into the URL in real time. Hit Share in the prompt bar to copy a link that restores the exact same state for anyone who opens it.
12 — Rewrite auto-generates
Opening the Rewrite & Tighten accordion when a prompt exists automatically generates all 5 variants. Each variant copy button turns green with ✓ Copied. History saves your last 20 prompts locally.
13 — Specificity beats completeness
A sharp tension + one strong location often beats all seven fields filled with mediocre choices. Fill what you care about and let AI interpret the rest.
14 — Recommended pairings
GLASS × real vs. reflected · STAGE × spotlight vs. shadow · UNDERWORLD × love vs. loss eternal · EXPEDITION × wonder vs. fear · CIRCUS × trust vs. fear · MONASTERY × devotion vs. doubt · STORM × force vs. shelter · FARM × seed vs. harvest · PIER × end vs. beginning · ROOFTOP × golden hour vs. blue hour
The 5 Rewrite Variants
1 — Cinematic Scale: Tension leads, pose fused with location.
2 — Intimacy: Pose leads, location frames, tension last as subtext.
3 — Essential: Tension as standalone statement, compressed.
4 — Location-Led: Location opens, tension and pose embedded.
5 — Wardrobe-Led: Look establishes visual register, tension follows.
