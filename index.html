Tips for Better Prompts
1 — Start with World & Tension
Step 2 opens automatically. Search 50 worlds or browse by category. Pick a tension — a poetic translation appears beneath it. Tension unlocks affinity-matched locations, poses and looks at the top of every dropdown.
2 — Tension is mood, not instruction
Every prompt ends with: "The emotional tension (X) is a mood and atmosphere directive only — do not illustrate it literally." This prevents AI models from rendering tensions literally.
3 — Lock & Spin Unlocked
Lock any field with the padlock. Hit Spin Unlocked to explore every combination within your locked anchor. Locks survive Randomize All and Clear.
4 — Keyboard shortcuts
Press / to jump to world search. Press 1 2 3 4 to toggle steps. Press Alt+R to randomize. Press Alt+C to copy. Press Escape to close modals.
5 — ✦ AI decides
Every field has a ✦ AI button. Setting 2–3 fields manually and leaving 1–2 to AI often produces the strongest results. The button spins briefly to confirm.
6 — Randomize All opens steps
Hit ✦ Randomize All and all four steps open showing what was selected. Lock what you like, Spin Unlocked to explore. Alt+R randomizes from anywhere.
7 — Prompt strip
The slim bar at the top shows your live prompt preview. Tap it to expand the full coloured prompt with coherence badge, Copy and Share. Tap the Copy button on the strip for a quick copy without expanding.
8 — Shareable links
Every selection encodes into the URL in real time. Hit Share in the prompt panel to copy a link restoring the exact same state for anyone who opens it. Browser back/forward navigates between states.
9 — Coherence badge
Green ✦ Cohesive / amber ◐ Moderate / red ○ Scattered scores your combination against the tension's affinity map. Tap or hover to see exactly why. Scattered can produce more distinctive images.
10 — Era filter
Era appends a period and palette modifier. 1970s on Mountain nudges earth tones and grain. Tap the active pill again — or hit the ✕ clear button — to deselect.
11 — Rewrite auto-generates
Opening Rewrite & Tighten when a prompt exists automatically generates all 5 variants. A ⚠ banner appears if selections change after generating. History saves your last 20 prompts — tap to expand, tap again to copy.
12 — Recommended pairings
GLASS × real vs. reflected · STAGE × spotlight vs. shadow · UNDERWORLD × love vs. loss eternal · EXPEDITION × wonder vs. fear · CIRCUS × trust vs. fear · MONASTERY × devotion vs. doubt · STORM × force vs. shelter · FARM × seed vs. harvest · PIER × end vs. beginning · ROOFTOP × golden hour vs. blue hour
The 5 Rewrite Variants
1 Cinematic Scale: Tension leads, pose fused with location.
2 Intimacy: Pose leads, location frames, tension last.
3 Essential: Compressed — tension as statement.
4 Location-Led: Location opens, tension embedded.
5 Wardrobe-Led: Look establishes register, tension follows.
How to Use Your Prompt
1
Choose World & Tension

2
Build the scene or Randomize

3
